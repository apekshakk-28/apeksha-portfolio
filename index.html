03 · Methodology

How I run an assessment

Every engagement follows the same disciplined sequence — this is the actual order of operations, not a stylistic device.

01

Reconnaissance

Map the attack surface — endpoints, services, and exposed assets — using Nmap and manual discovery across network, web, and mobile targets.

02

Scanning & Enumeration

Probe identified surfaces with Burp Suite, Postman, and MobSF to enumerate parameters, permissions, and app behavior in detail.

03

Exploitation & Validation

Test authentication, authorization, session handling, and input validation against the OWASP Top 10 and OWASP Mobile Top 10 to confirm real, exploitable issues.

04

Reporting & Remediation

Document each finding with severity rating, proof of concept, business impact, and clear remediation guidance the client's team can act on.
